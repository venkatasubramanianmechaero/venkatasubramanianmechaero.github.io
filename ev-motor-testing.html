Project 03 • EV Powertrain End-of-Line Role: Supporting Team Member in Designing Test Rigs & Conveyors

EV Motor Transaxle Test Rigs

An EV motor transaxle test rig is a specialized, high-precision laboratory and production system designed to evaluate the performance, durability, and efficiency of electric vehicle powertrain components.

Fig 1. EV Motor & Transaxle Drivetrain Unit Under Test (UUT)

Steps of Process in End of Line Testing

01 Loading of EV motor Transaxle in Pallet
02 Oil Filling using Quick Release Coupling
03 Manual Shaft engage onto the EV motor
04 Transfer From Conveyor 2 to Conveyor 1 using Transfer Table
05 Test Rig Loading two sides of the shaft using Dynamometers with Power supplied on motor of UUT
06 After testing the UUT, pallet transfer from Conveyor 1 to Conveyor 3
07 Waiting Station is provided in Conveyor 2 and Conveyor 3
08 In oil draining, oil drain port is unplugged from Unit Under Test, and a separate oil drain setup is provided to suction the dirty oil
