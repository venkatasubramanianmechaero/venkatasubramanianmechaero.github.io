Differential Line Assembly – OEM

A mechanical differential is a gear system in vehicles that allows drive wheels to rotate at different speeds while cornering, ensuring stability, traction, and reduced tire wear.

Fig 1. Crown Gear, Pinion Shaft & Spider Gear Differential Assembly

Components Used

Differential Line

Crown Gear, Pinion Shaft, Differential Housing, Bearings, Ring Nut, Seal, Springs, Shim, and Spider Gear.

Whap Axle Line

Precision axle alignment, sub-assembly integration, and structural support stations.

Shaft Connection

Spline coupling and mechanical shaft engagement for torque transmission and EOL verification.
